# Adaptation by project type

These questions are added to the base sections of prompts 1 to 7. Never remove a base section.
Insert each addition into the file indicated.

## Contents
- Website or redesign
- App or digital product
- Brand identity
- Content or communication project
- Other type of project

## Website or redesign
- **02-project**: current website (address, what works, what does not work), pages that are essential, existing content that can be reused.
- **03-customers**: what visitors look for first, where they come from (search, social media, word of mouth).
- **04-market**: competitor or reference websites, and what they do better.
- **07-constraints**: hosting and domain name, required content management system, existing search ranking to preserve, accessibility, legal notices and consent.

## App or digital product
- **02-project**: the problem solved in one sentence, the minimum version expected, target platforms, untested assumptions.
- **03-customers**: users and buyers (are they the same people?), context of use, current alternatives.
- **06-pains-doubts**: what has already been tried technically, technical debt or existing tools.
- **07-constraints**: integrations, data handled, security, compliance requirements, available technical team.

## Brand identity
- **05-brand**: go deeper (origin of the name, symbols already in use, what must survive the change).
- **04-market**: the visual codes of the sector, and what competitors have too much in common.
- **06-pains-doubts**: what bothers people about the current identity, earlier redesign attempts.
- **07-constraints**: required variations (media, signage, vehicles, packaging), ownership of the existing assets.

## Content or communication project
- **02-project**: target channels, realistic frequency, who produces and who approves.
- **03-customers**: topics that interest them, formats they consume.
- **05-brand**: examples of content that worked and content that failed.
- **07-constraints**: time available internally, legal approvals, publishing tools.

## Other type of project
Start from the base sections, then ask yourself, for each file, which piece of information a provider in this trade most regrets not having had at the start. Add 2 to 4 questions at most per file.
