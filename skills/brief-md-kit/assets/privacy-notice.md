## Before you start: what you share

These files describe your company, your project and sometimes your doubts. Here is how you stay in control:

- **You reread everything before sending it.** Nothing is sent without your consent, and you can delete, correct or rephrase any line.
- **No names of people.** The prompts ask for roles ("the managing director", "the technical lead"). Check that no private name has slipped into the files.
- **No unnecessary personal data.** No health data, no personal financial situation, and no information about your customers or employees that would make it possible to identify them.
- **File 06 is confidential by nature.** It talks about your doubts. It is meant only for {{PROVIDER}}, and you can decide not to send it or to share it only during a meeting.
- **Your AI assistant has its own rules.** Check in its settings what it does with your conversations (how long they are kept, whether they are used for training) before you trust it with sensitive content.

### Sector paragraph (to adapt)
- **Health, legal, finance, education:** do not include any data about patients, clients, cases or students, even partly anonymized. Describe categories, never individual cases.
- **Other sectors:** if a confidentiality agreement binds you to partners, do not include their information.
