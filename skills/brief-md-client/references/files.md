# Brief MD files: expected sections

*The headings below must match `scripts/spec.json` exactly, because `scripts/brief_lint.py` checks them: change both files together, never one alone.*

The level 2 headings are technical keys read by the checking script, so keep them exactly as they are, in English, even when the content is written in another language. The hints after each heading guide the content and are not copied into the headings.

The sections marked **[blocking]** match a field without which the provider cannot frame the project, so handle them first.

## 02-project.md (levels: express, standard)

- **Goal in one sentence** **[blocking]**
- **Why now**: the trigger
- **Scope: included**
- **Scope: excluded**
- **Expected deliverables**
- **Success criteria** **[blocking]**: measurable if possible
- **Deadlines and milestones** **[blocking]**
- **Budget** **[blocking]**: a range or a ceiling, otherwise [unknown]
- **Final decision-maker and stakeholders** **[blocking]**: by role
- **History**: previous attempts, what was done or abandoned
- **Dependencies**: what the project depends on
- **Questions to complete**: 3 at most

## 03-customers.md (levels: express, standard)

- **Segments** **[blocking]**: 2 to 4 at most
- **What customers say**: real quotes only, flag any paraphrase
- **Customers the company does not want to target**
- **Current journey**: from discovery to purchase or order
- **Questions to complete**: 3 at most

## 07-constraints.md (levels: express, standard)

- **Budget and deadlines**: a reminder
- **Technical constraints**: required tools, hosting, integrations
- **Legal and regulatory constraints**: personal data, accessibility, legal notices, intellectual property
- **Available internal resources**: time and people, by role
- **Required approvals**: who approves what, and how quickly
- **Non-negotiables**
- **Access and items to provide to the provider**
- **Questions to complete**: 3 at most

## 06-pains-doubts.md (levels: express, standard)

- **Current frustrations**
- **What failed before, and why**
- **Doubts about this project**
- **Fears**: budget, deadline, loss of control, disappointing result
- **Sensitive topics or internal tensions**: by role, without naming anyone
- **What is rarely said in meetings**
- **Questions to complete**: 3 at most

## 01-company.md (levels: standard)

- **Identity**: name, legal form, year founded, size, location
- **Business in one sentence**
- **Offer**: products or services, from most to least important
- **Business model**: how the company makes money
- **Key figures**: order of magnitude and year for revenue, headcount, growth, volume
- **Organization and decision-making**: who decides what, by role
- **History and key moments**
- **Values: stated and actually lived**: tell the two apart
- **Current tools and environment**
- **Questions to complete**: 3 at most

## 04-market.md (levels: standard)

- **Current positioning**
- **Direct competitors**: 3 to 5, named
- **Indirect competitors and substitutes**
- **What sets us apart**: with evidence, otherwise [inferred]
- **Industry trends and threats**
- **Admired references**: and why
- **Seasonality or business rhythm**
- **Questions to complete**: 3 at most

## 05-brand.md (levels: standard)

- **Mission and promise**
- **Personality**: 5 adjectives at most
- **Tone of voice**: with 2 example sentences that show it
- **What the brand is not**
- **Existing assets**: logo, brand guidelines, typefaces, colors: yes or no, and where to find them
- **Content that represents the brand well**: links or descriptions
- **Words to use and words to avoid**
- **Questions to complete**: 3 at most

## 00-summary.md (levels: standard)

- **Index**: each file with a one-sentence summary
- **The 5 most important points**
- **Information to verify**: every high-impact [inferred] and [memory] mention
- **Missing information**: every [unknown] mention
- **Sensitive data possibly present**: to remove before sending if not needed
